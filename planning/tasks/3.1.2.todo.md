## Notes
- `crates/newhall-core/src/thermal_1d.rs:960` calls `pub(crate) fn classify_smr(...)` directly, passing `dif = msst - mwst`. Keeping `classify_smr`'s signature and behavior unchanged preserves both `ClassicThermal` and `Thermal1DModel`. `StrictThermal` passes `(st - wt).abs() * config.amplitude` into `classify_smr` (ensuring attenuated magnitude `dif` in Xeric/Udic/Ustic branches per Defect `#4`).
- `crates/newhall-core/src/types.rs` defines `ThermalClassification` with distinct typed enums `regime_subdivision1: RegimeSubdivision1` (`q` variants: `Typic`, `Weak`, `Wet`, `Dry`, `Extreme`, `Xeric`, `Udic`, `Aridic`, `Undefined`) and `regime_subdivision2: RegimeSubdivision2` (`div` variants: `Aridic`, `Tempustic`, `Tropustic`, `Tempudic`, `Xeric`, `Udic`, `Tropudic`, `Undefined`), and `types.rs` is outside scope. Thus `StrictThermal` preserves Rust enum type safety and documents the `q`/`div` field mapping rather than breaking enum types (Defect `#8`).
- All `StrictThermal` state stays stack-allocated and `Copy` with zero heap allocations.
- Only `crates/newhall-core/src/thermal.rs` will be modified in the crate.
